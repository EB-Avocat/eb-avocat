## Context

The audit recommends keeping the logo, the teal palette, Museo and the tone (§5). Open points: the Museo web licence is not recorded and the OTFs are served unoptimised (A-28); imagery is one portrait (§5 "Question"); the brand looks like a template (A-08).

## Scope

- Confirm the Museo web licence with Eva (or the foundry); if not covered, choose a fallback or a licence to buy.
- Decide what evolves: logo use, palette extensions (accent, neutrals warmed toward teal), imagery style (documentary photography, illustration, none), iconography.
- Moodboard and 2-3 directions in Figcraft, one chosen by Eva.
- Output: brand rules page in Figcraft (`Foundations v2`).

## Out of scope

Token definitions and components (design system issue), page layouts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Museo licence status recorded; font plan decided (WOFF2, weights kept).
- [ ] Moodboard and chosen direction approved by Eva.
- [ ] Rules for logo, colour, imagery, icons documented.
- [ ] Photography needs listed (portrait, office, details), with a plan to obtain them.
